The galaxy is locked. You hold the key.

In 2128 the Vorr Hive Dominion sealed every free world inside a Cube Seal, a living prison made of glowing arrows. Only one pilot can read the seal language: you. Rotate the 3D cube, find the arrows that have a clear path and tap them in the right order to shoot them free.
