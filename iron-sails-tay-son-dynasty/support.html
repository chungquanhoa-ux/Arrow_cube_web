Support

Iron Sails: Tay Son Dynasty · iPhone

Need help? Email Dolotech.vn@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, game version (Settings › About) and a short description or screenshot. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

I bought a flag or ship but don't see it.

Open Settings or the Flag/Shipyard screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I get ship blueprints?

Watch an optional rewarded ad at the Shipyard to receive one blueprint piece; collect the required pieces to unlock the next ship tier. Daily limits apply.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The Game plays offline. An internet connection is only needed to load rewarded ads and to make or restore purchases.

How do I change the language?

Settings › Language. Available: English, Tiếng Việt, 中文, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Bahasa Indonesia.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

All game data is stored on your device. Deleting the Game removes it. See our Privacy Policy.
