Iron Sails:
Tay Son Dynasty

Take the helm of a Tay Son war junk on the sea of Thị Nại – Quy Nhơn, 1790–1792. Chase pirates, ram enemy ships, and raise your own coastal citadel behind 18th-century Vietnamese walls.

Features

Command a war junk

Sail freely across a hand-crafted sea of lagoons, limestone karsts, sandbars and deserted islands.

Broadside, bow gun & ramming

Fire broadsides, mount a bow cannon, reinforce your prow and ram enemy ships until they break apart.

Build your citadel

Grow a fishing post into a walled citadel on a white-sand peninsula: workshops, farms, trading house, ramparts and gate.

Story-driven campaign

Daily jobs, pirate hunts and a story set in the Tay Son era. Plays fully offline.

Fly your flag

The Tay Son and Vietnam flags are free. Other flags can be unlocked and fly over your citadel and your ship.

7 languages

English, Tiếng Việt, 中文, Deutsch, Français, Italiano and Bahasa Indonesia.
