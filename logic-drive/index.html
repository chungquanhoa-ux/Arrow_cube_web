The lot is jammed. The passengers are waiting.

Tap a car that has a clear way out and it drives along the arrow on its roof into a parking slot. Passengers ride around the conveyor belt; when one passes a parked car of the same color, they hop in. Fill every seat and the car drives away. Clear the whole lot to win.

Endless levels

New colors and trickier lots as you go, with a fresh theme every few levels.

Ice cars, rainbow buses, Rush

Ice cars need two taps, rainbow buses take any passenger and Rush levels race the clock.

Boosters and rewards

Earn stars and coins, claim a daily bonus and use Magnet or Rainbow when you are stuck.

Play your way

Four free parking slots, four more with an optional ad or coins. Works offline, no sign-in.

Giới thiệu

Logic Drive: Conveyor Jam là trò chơi giải đố bãi đỗ xe 3D dễ thương. Chạm vào xe có đường thoát để đưa xe vào ô đỗ, rồi để hành khách trên băng chuyền lên đúng xe cùng màu. Xe đủ khách sẽ chạy đi. Giải tỏa cả bãi để qua màn. Miễn phí, không cần tài khoản, chơi được khi không có mạng.
