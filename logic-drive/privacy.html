Logic Drive: Conveyor Jam (the "Game") is published by Dolotech Technology Company Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game uses and who receives it. Contact: dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device only

The Game stores the following on your device. We do not receive it, and it is not sent to our servers:

  • your progress: the level you reached, your coins, how many parking slots you have unlocked, and your daily-bonus streak and the day you last played;
  • your settings: sound and music on or off.

Deleting the Game deletes this information. The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in and no online profile.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and supported by Google AdMob rewarded video ads. An ad is shown only when you tap an optional button: to unlock an extra parking slot, to get 50 bonus coins, or to continue after a level is lost. The Game has no banner ads and no ads that start by themselves. The Game has no in-app purchases and you cannot spend real money in it.

To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device identifiers (including the advertising identifier on iOS and Android), IP address and approximate location, how you interact with ads and the Game, and crash and performance data.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Your consent and tracking choices

When the Game starts, and before any ad is requested, it may show:

  • a Google consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) where privacy law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland;
  • on iPhone and iPad, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, which asks whether the Game may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you more relevant ads.

You can say no. You can still play the whole Game for free; ads are then not personalised. Where privacy law applies, the "Privacy choices" button in the About screen (the "i" button) lets you review your choice at any time. You can also change tracking permission in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking, or manage ads in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data because you use the Game is Google (advertising, section 2). Apple and Google also process data when you download the Game from their stores, under their own policies.

6. Your choices

  • Delete all data stored by the Game by deleting the Game from your device.
  • Review your consent with "Privacy choices" in the About screen (where shown), or in your device settings as described in section 3.
  • You can contact us with any question or request at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
