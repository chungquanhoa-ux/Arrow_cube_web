Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Galactic Arrow 3D (the "Game") is published by Dolotech Technology Company Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game uses and who receives it. Contact: dolotech.vn@gmail.comL.

1. Information stored on your device only

The Game stores the following on your device. We do not receive it, and it is not sent to our servers:

  • the display name, age and country you enter in your player profile;
  • your progress (level reached, lives, hints, best level, arrows solved) and which story chapters you have read;
  • your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, graphics quality).

Deleting the Game deletes this information. The Game has no user accounts.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the top of the screen, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch (to continue after losing, or to get a hint).

To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, how you interact with ads and the Game, and crash and performance data. The Game does not ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not read Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. In-app purchases

The Game offers an optional purchase of a pack of 20 hints. Payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store). We do not receive or store your payment details. The Game receives confirmation from the App Store that the purchase completed so that the hints can be added.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The age you enter in the profile stays on your device.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third parties that receive data because you use the Game are Google (advertising, section 2) and Apple (purchases, section 3).

6. Your choices

  • Delete all data stored by the Game by deleting the Game from your device, or reset progress with "Restart" in Settings.
  • On iOS you can limit ad personalisation in Settings, Privacy & Security, Apple Advertising.
  • You can contact us with any question or request at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
