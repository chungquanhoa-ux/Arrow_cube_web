Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

In short: Moto Rush: City Rider Game ("the app") is an offline game. There is no account or login, your game progress is saved only on your device, and we do not run any server that stores personal data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob: Google collects some device data to deliver ads, and uses it for personalised ads only with your consent. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

1. Who we are

The app is developed and published by Dolotech Technology Company Limited, Vietnam ("Dolotech", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for the processing described in this policy. Contact: dolotech.vn@gmail.com. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through its advertising services (section 3).

2. Data stored only on your device

Game progress (coins, gems, unlocks, scores, quests, the local leaderboard, settings such as language, sound and reminders, and whether you bought Remove Ads) is saved in the app's private storage on your device and is never sent to us. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location, and the app has no sign-in. Your device may include this data in your own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup), which you control.

3. Advertising: Google AdMob and Google consent (UMP)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for and store your consent where the law requires it. When an ad is requested, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers: on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt — and the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV); on Android the Advertising ID, subject to your consent and device settings;
  • IP address, from which Google derives an approximate (coarse) location such as your country or city;
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, views, taps and whether a rewarded ad was completed;
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance data of the ads SDK, plus technical information such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • your consent choices, which UMP stores on your device and sends with ad requests.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and abuse, and — only if you consent — show personalised ads. If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads, and you can still play for free. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store / StoreKit) or Google (Google Play Billing) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your payment card, name or billing address. The app only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded so it can deliver the item, and keeps that record on your device ("Restore Purchases" asks Apple or Google for it again).

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (daily reward, full chest, come-back reminders). No push server is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

6. No analytics, no selling data for money

We do not use any analytics or tracking SDK other than the data Google's ads SDK processes as described above. We do not sell personal data for money.

7. If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include (for example your device model or a screenshot). We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed to handle your request. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent — personalised ads, tracking across apps (ATT on iOS), and storing or accessing information on your device for ads where the law requires consent.
  • Legitimate interests — showing non-personalised ads to keep the game free, fraud prevention and security, ad measurement and diagnostics, and answering your emails. You can object at any time.
  • Contract — delivering and restoring the items you buy.

9. Your choices

  • In the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices (privacy options) opens Google's consent form, where you can change or withdraw your consent and, in US states with privacy laws, opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. The button is shown in regions where these choices apply (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states).
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — turn tracking off for the app at any time.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) — delete or reset your Advertising ID.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before. You can keep playing whatever you choose.

10. International transfers

Google LLC is based in the United States, and Google may process data in the US and other countries. For data from the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) where it is certified, and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (with the UK addendum). We are based in Vietnam; the only personal data we handle ourselves is email you send us.

11. Retention

  • Game progress stays on your device until you delete the app (or clear its data).
  • We hold no personal data on our servers. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies: How Google retains data.

12. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time (Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular where you live or work (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). Because we do not hold game or advertising data linked to you, we cannot look it up ourselves; we will help you use the controls above and Google's tools. Write to dolotech.vn@gmail.com; we reply within one month.

13. US state privacy rights

Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other US states with privacy laws have these additional rights.

  • What is collected (by Google through the ads SDK): identifiers (device advertising IDs, IP address), internet or network activity (ad interactions), approximate geolocation derived from the IP address, and diagnostic data. We do not collect sensitive personal information.
  • Purposes and recipients: advertising, ad measurement, security and fraud prevention, and diagnostics; disclosed to Google as our advertising partner.
  • Sale, sharing and targeted advertising: we do not sell personal data for money. Letting Google use your device identifiers for personalised ads may count as a "sale", "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. You can opt out at any time with the Ad privacy choices (privacy options) button in the game's Settings, which offers Google's "Do not sell or share my personal information" choice; on iPhone you can also deny tracking in the ATT prompt or in device settings.
  • Your rights: to know/access, delete, correct and obtain a copy of your data, and to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. You may use an authorised agent. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; if you disagree with the outcome you may contact your state Attorney General.

14. Children

The app is a general-audience game (App Store age rating 4+). It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We tell Google that our ad requests are not child-directed and limit ads to general-audience content (rating G). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

15. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage, protected by your device's operating system. Data the ads SDK sends to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

16. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the app or the law changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date; if the changes are significant we will also mention them in the app's release notes.

17. Contact

Tiến trình chơi (xu, kim cương, nội dung đã mở khóa, điểm, nhiệm vụ, bảng xếp hạng cục bộ, cài đặt như ngôn ngữ, âm thanh, nhắc nhở và việc bạn đã mua Xóa quảng cáo hay chưa) được lưu trong vùng lưu trữ riêng của ứng dụng trên thiết bị và không bao giờ gửi cho chúng tôi. Google dùng dữ liệu này để phân phối và đo lường quảng cáo, giới hạn số lần lặp lại một quảng cáo, chống gian lận và lạm dụng, và — chỉ khi bạn đồng ý — hiển thị quảng cáo cá nhân hóa.
