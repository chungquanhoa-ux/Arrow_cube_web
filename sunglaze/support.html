Support & FAQ

Need a hand restoring a window? Find quick answers below, or write to us – we read every message.

Playing the game

How does light mixing work?

Each piece is transparent coloured glass. Where two pieces overlap, their light combines: yellow + blue = green, red + blue = violet and red + yellow = orange. The faint drawing on the workbench shows the colour each cell needs. Two pieces of the same colour can't overlap. When every cell has the right colour, the sun shines through and the window is restored.

What are the controls?
  • Drag a piece of glass into the frame to place it.
  • Tap a piece to turn it. Turning pieces in the tray never costs a move.
  • With a mouse or trackpad (for example on a Mac), right-click while dragging a piece to turn it.
How do hints and the prism lens work?

A hint shows a glowing outline where one piece belongs; tap the hint again to set the piece in place. You get 3 free hints every day, can earn more by watching an optional short video, buy hint packs in the Shop, or get unlimited daily hints with Premium. Purchased hints never expire. A prism lens reveals which colours of light every cell of the whole window needs.

Can I undo moves? How do I get stars?

Yes – tap Undo whenever you like. There is no timer and no way to lose. The top of the screen shows your moves and the goal for the window; the closer you stay to the goal, the more stars you earn. You can replay any window to improve your stars.

What do cracked, etched, mirror and dichroic glass do?
  • Cracked glass stays where you set it in the frame. Only Undo can lift it out again – handle with care.
  • Etched glass has a pattern that only reads one way, so it can't be turned.
  • Mirror glass appears twice: wherever you set it, its reflection appears on the other side of the window.
  • Dichroic glass changes colour with the angle: lying flat it shows one colour, standing upright it shows another.
  • You will also meet frosted glass (it hides the colour behind it, and every frosted cell needs frosted glass), prism glass (it splits light into red, yellow and blue in a row), fused glass (several colours fused into one piece) and antique panes that are fixed in the frame.
When does the Window of the Day change? Does it depend on my time zone?

The Window of the Day is made from the date, so everyone gets the same window on the same day – and it works offline. It uses the date on your device, so a new window appears at midnight in your local time zone. The bonus window opens with a short optional video when you are online, or at any time with Premium.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Sunglaze works fully offline – all puzzles, the Window of the Day and your progress are available without a connection. When you are offline, ads do not load and the ad buttons are hidden.

Can colour-blind players play?

Yes. Turn on colour symbols in Settings: every colour and every mix gets its own small symbol, so you can solve every window without relying on colour alone.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings > Language in the game and choose one of the 10 available languages, or pick Use device language.

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings or in the Shop. Premium and the window collections (Winter Lights, Secret Garden, Ocean Deep) are restored. Consumable items such as hints and prism lenses cannot be restored once used or if the app was deleted.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple according to its policies. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Notifications, privacy and data

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Use the Reminders switch in the game's Settings, or go to iOS Settings > Notifications > Sunglaze. Reminders (today's window is ready, and gentle reminders after 3 and 7 days) are created on your device – not by a server.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings > Ad privacy choices in the game to review or change your consent. On iOS you can also control tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Owning Premium removes interstitial ads. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Sunglaze has no account and stores everything on your device. Deleting the app removes all progress, settings and local data. To reset advertising tracking, use your device's tracking or advertising settings. If you have emailed us and want your messages deleted, just ask.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Progress is saved only on your device and is not synced through our servers (we have none). Your device's own backup and restore may carry app data to a new device. Non-consumable purchases can always be restored with Restore Purchases.
