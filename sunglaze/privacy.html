Privacy Policy

Sunglaze: Stained Glass Puzzle · App ID com.chung.sunglaze
Effective date: 15 November 2026

In short

  • Sunglaze works fully offline. There is no account and no login, and we run no servers that receive your data.
  • Your progress, settings and purchases are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and we have built no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own into the game.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may process some device and usage data to show and measure ads, according to the choices you make in the consent message and in Apple's tracking prompt.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Who is responsible

Sunglaze: Stained Glass Puzzle (“Sunglaze”, “the app”) is developed and published by DOLOTECH, the brand of an independent developer based in Vietnam (“we”, “us”). We are the controller for the limited processing described in this policy that is under our control. You can reach us at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Sunglaze app on iOS (iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo) and, once released, on Android, and to this website.

2. What we do not collect

Sunglaze is designed to work without us knowing who you are. We do not ask for, collect or store:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there is no account or login);
  • your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files;
  • your precise or approximate location;
  • analytics, usage statistics or crash reports – the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

We do not operate any game servers. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following locally on your device only: puzzle progress and stars, your album, town decorations, Lumens and other in-game items, settings (such as language, sound, colour symbols and notification preferences) and a local record of purchased items. This data never leaves your device through us. It is removed when you delete the app. Depending on your device settings, your device's own backup (for example an iCloud device backup) may include app data; such backups are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy, not by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sunglaze is free to play and is supported by ads delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited (“Google”).

How ads appear

  • Rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch one – for example to get extra hints, to open the bonus Window of the Day, or to double the Lumens you earned.
  • Interstitial ads are rare and appear only between puzzles – never during a puzzle, and never for players who own Premium.
  • Ads load only when your device is online. When you are offline, the ad buttons are hidden and the game continues to work normally.
  • We limit ad content to a maximum rating of PG and block sensitive ad categories.

What Google may process

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers – on iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise Google uses non-tracking identifiers such as the identifier for vendors as permitted by Apple;
  • your IP address, and a coarse (approximate) location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data, such as ads shown, clicks and video completions;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and app ID.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and – where you have consented – personalise ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google acts under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Consent and ad privacy choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Before ads are requested, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent message, which is based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF). You can accept, reject or customise the purposes and vendors. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still serve non-personalised or limited ads as described in the message.
  • United States: Where required by US state privacy laws, the app shows a US state privacy message through the same platform, which lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Apple tracking prompt (ATT): On iOS, the IDFA is only available if you choose “Allow” in Apple's prompt. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Change your mind: You can review or change your choices at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy choices.

6. In-app purchases

Sunglaze offers optional purchases through the Apple App Store (and Google Play once the Android version is released): a non-consumable Premium unlock (no ads, unlimited daily hints, double Lumens and the bonus Night Market building), three non-consumable window collections (Winter Lights, Secret Garden, Ocean Deep), and consumable packs (hints, prism lenses and a one-time Starter set). There are no subscriptions and no loot boxes.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your name, payment card or billing details. The app receives only a confirmation from the store that a product was purchased, which is stored locally so the item can be unlocked. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy. You can restore non-consumable purchases with Restore Purchases in Settings or in the Shop.

7. Local notifications

If you allow it, Sunglaze can show an optional daily reminder that today's window is ready, and gentle reminders if you have not played for 3 or 7 days. These notifications are scheduled on your device by the app itself – they are not sent from a server, and no notification token is shared with us. We ask for permission at a sensible moment rather than on first launch, and you can switch reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

8. App Store ratings

The app may occasionally invite you to rate it using Apple's official in-app review API. Apple controls how often this prompt appears, and any rating or review you submit is handled by Apple. We do not receive personal data from this prompt.

9. When you email us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message (for example your device model, iOS version and app version). We use this only to answer you and to fix problems, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support (or to take steps at your request). We do not use it for marketing and do not share it, except with our email provider, which stores the messages.

10. Children

Sunglaze has an App Store age rating of 4+ and its content is suitable for players of all ages. However, the app is not directed at children under 13 (or the corresponding minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app itself collects no personal information, there is no account or profile to create. Ads are requested as not child-directed, but with ad content limited to a maximum rating of PG and sensitive categories blocked. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information (for example by email), please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection apply, the processing described here relies on:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) – for personalised advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising purposes, collected through the consent message and, on iOS, the ATT prompt; and for local notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) – for providing the game and delivering items you purchase.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) – for showing non-personalised ads to fund a free game, for fraud prevention by the ad provider, and for answering support emails.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) – where we must retain records required by law.

12. Your rights in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold almost no data about you, most requests can be met by the controls in the app (Ad privacy choices), by your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can also exercise your rights directly with Google. To exercise any right with us, email dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work – for example your national data protection authority in the EU, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK, or the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) in Switzerland.

13. United States privacy rights (California and other states)

This section applies to residents of California (CCPA as amended by the CPRA) and other US states with comprehensive privacy laws (for example Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon).

  • Categories of personal information: through the Google Mobile Ads SDK – identifiers (such as advertising ID where permitted and IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (ad interactions), and approximate geolocation derived from IP address. If you email us: your email address and message contents.
  • Sources: your device, via the ad SDK; you, when you email us.
  • Purposes: showing and measuring ads, preventing fraud, and providing support.
  • No sale by us: We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.
  • Sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising: When Google processes data to show personalised ads, this may be considered “sharing” (or “sale” or “targeted advertising” under some state laws). You can opt out by choosing to opt out in the US state privacy message, at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices, by not allowing tracking in Apple's ATT prompt, and by limiting ad tracking in your device settings. We also honour Global Privacy Control signals where they are technically received by the app.
  • Your rights: to know or access, to delete, to correct, to opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect any), and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. You can appeal a decision by replying to our response. Authorised agents may submit requests on your behalf with proof of authorisation.
  • Retention: see section 16.

14. Brazil (LGPD)

If you are in Brazil, the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (Law No. 13,709/2018) gives you the rights to confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about the entities with which data is shared, information about the possibility of refusing consent and its consequences, and revocation of consent. The legal bases we rely on are consent (for personalised ads), performance of a contract and legitimate interest, as described in section 11. You can contact us at dolotech.vn@gmail.com, and you may file a complaint with the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

15. Japan (APPI)

For users in Japan, the following information is provided under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI):

  • Business operator: DOLOTECH (independent developer based in Vietnam). Contact: dolotech.vn@gmail.com.
  • Purpose of use: delivering and measuring ads within the app, preventing ad fraud, and responding to enquiries.
  • Provision to third parties: Device identifiers, IP address and ad interaction data may be provided to Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Where this involves personal data or personally referable information, it takes place on the basis of your consent given in the consent message and/or Apple's tracking prompt.
  • Cross-border transfer: Google may process this information in the United States and other countries. The United States does not have a comprehensive federal privacy law equivalent to the APPI; Google has stated that it protects data with measures such as its privacy policy and contractual commitments. Information on the personal data protection systems of foreign countries is published by Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC).
  • Security control measures: see section 18.
  • Requests: You may request notification of the purpose of use, disclosure, correction, suspension of use, deletion, or cessation of third-party provision of retained personal data by emailing us. We will verify the request and reply without undue delay. Note that we generally do not hold personal data that identifies you, other than emails you send us.

16. Retention

  • Game data on your device: kept until you delete the app (or reset your device).
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed to resolve your request, and generally deleted within 24 months, unless longer retention is required by law.
  • Advertising data processed by Google: retained according to Google's own retention policies.

17. International transfers

We are based in Vietnam. Google may process ad-related data in the United States and other countries. For transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google relies on recognised safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Support emails are stored by our email provider, which may also process data in other countries.

18. Security

Because we do not run servers or collect account data, the main safeguard is that your game data never leaves your device through us. We regularly update the app and its third-party SDKs, request only the device permissions we need (notifications, if you choose), and protect the email account we use for support with strong authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the small amount of data involved.

19. Deleting your data

  • Game data: uninstall Sunglaze. All progress, settings and local purchase records are deleted from the device. (Non-consumable purchases can be restored later with Restore Purchases; consumable items and Lumens cannot be recovered.)
  • Advertising ID: on iOS, turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track or deny tracking for Sunglaze in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android (once available), you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Ad choices: change them in the game under Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • Emails: ask us to delete your correspondence at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

20. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date and, for significant changes, inform you in the app where appropriate. If a change requires your consent, we will ask for it.
