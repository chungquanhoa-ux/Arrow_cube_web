Sunglaze: Stained Glass Puzzle

Welcome to Saltglass Bay, a quiet seaside town whose coloured windows have lost their glow. Layer transparent panes of glass, let the colours mix, and watch the sunlight pour through once more.

Light is your paintbrush

Every window starts as a faint drawing on the workbench. Drag pieces of transparent coloured glass into the frame and turn them until they cover the drawing. Where pieces overlap, the light mixes:

  • += yellow + blue = green
  • += red + blue = violet
  • += red + yellow = orange

Give every cell the colour it needs and the sun shines through, filling the room with colour.

Made for calm moments

No pressure, no countdowns – just you, the glass and the light.

300+ puzzles

Every window is carefully crafted with our puzzle generator and checked to have exactly one solution.

Special glass

Frosted, etched, cracked, prism, mirror, dichroic and fused glass – plus antique panes fixed in place – bring new twists.

Window of the Day

A fresh window every day, the same for everyone, made from the date – it even works offline.

Seasonal festivals

Celebrate the seasons in Saltglass Bay with special festival windows throughout the year.

Day & night album

Every finished window is saved to your album, glowing by day and by night.

Decorate the town

Friendly for every player

  • No timer and no way to lose. Take as long as you like.
  • Undo any move, and use step-by-step hints whenever you feel stuck.
  • Colour symbols on every pane help colour-blind players tell the glass apart.

  • Available in 10 languages.
