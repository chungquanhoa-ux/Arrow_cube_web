Privacy Policy

Lodestar: Galactic Fleet War · App ID com.chung.lodestarlastfleet
Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy is also available in Tiếng Việt. If the two versions differ, the English version prevails to the extent permitted by law.

In short

  • Lodestar is a single-player game. There is no account, no sign-in, no chat and no user-generated content, and we run no servers that receive your data.
  • Your progress, settings and purchase records are saved only on your device. There is no cloud save.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location, and we have added no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads, never during a battle. Google may process some device and usage data to show and measure ads, according to the choices you make in the consent message and, on iPhone and iPad, in Apple's tracking prompt.
  • Purchases and the Admiral Pass subscription are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who is responsible

Lodestar: Galactic Fleet War (“Lodestar”, “the game”, “the app”) is developed and published by DOLOTECH TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD, based in Vietnam (“we”, “us”). We are the controller for the limited processing described in this policy that is under our control. You can reach us at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Lodestar app for Android devices (distributed through Google Play) and for iPhone and iPad (distributed through the App Store), both with the app ID com.chung.lodestarlastfleet, and to this website. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts, and loads no external fonts or images.

2. Overview and what we do not collect

Lodestar is designed to work without us knowing who you are. We do not ask for, collect or store:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there is no account or sign-in);
  • your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files;
  • your precise location;
  • chat messages or user-generated content (the game is single-player, with no chat and no online multiplayer);
  • analytics, usage statistics or crash reports – the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following locally on your device only: your progress (missions, story choices, ships, upgrades, Salvage and Lumen Cores), your settings (such as the language), a local record of your purchases (including the end date of an active Admiral Pass), and simple counters used to apply daily limits to rewards and ads. There is no cloud save and no server of ours, so this data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app. Depending on your device settings, your device's own backup (on Android, by Google; on iPhone and iPad, for example an iCloud backup) may include app data; such backups are governed by Google's or Apple's terms and privacy policies, not by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lodestar is free to play and is supported by ads delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited (“Google”), through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

How ads appear

  • Rewarded ads are optional. An ad plays only when you tap a button to watch one, for a reward shown before you choose – for example extra Salvage after a mission, a few Lumen Cores or a second chance. Rewarded ads are limited per day.
  • Interstitial ads (full-screen ads) may appear occasionally between missions, after the results screen. They never appear during a battle, and their frequency is limited.
  • There are no banner ads. The Remove ads pack and the Admiral Pass switch off interstitial ads and give you the rewards of rewarded ads without watching an ad.
  • Ads load only when your device is online.

What Google may process

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers – on Android, the Android Advertising ID (AAID), which you can reset or delete in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device); on iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, otherwise the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and other non-tracking identifiers permitted by Apple;
  • your IP address and an approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data, such as ads shown, taps and video completions;
  • device and app information and diagnostics, such as device model, operating-system version, language, app version, and ad performance and crash data of the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, to personalise ads where you have consented, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google acts under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Consent, tracking and privacy options

  • Consent message (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): Before ads are requested, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You can accept, reject or choose individual purposes and vendors. Where a US state privacy message applies, the same platform lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Apple tracking prompt (ATT, iPhone and iPad): The app asks for permission to track only through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The IDFA is available only if you choose “Allow”. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: There is no ATT prompt on Android. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may use the Android Advertising ID (AAID). You can reset or delete it at any time in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Non-personalised ads: If you do not give consent, or (on iPhone and iPad) you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalised based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which rely on context such as the app and your approximate location.
  • Change your mind: Where a consent choice applies to you, an Ad privacy button in the Store tab of the Hangar lets you review or change your choices at any time. It works the same way on Android, iPhone and iPad.

6. In-app purchases and subscription

Lodestar offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing (Android) and the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad), handled in the app by Unity IAP:

  • Lumen Cores packs – consumable: used up when you spend them, and cannot be restored;
  • the Starter pack and Remove ads – non-consumable: bought once and restorable;
  • the Admiral Pass – a monthly auto-renewing subscription.

There are no loot boxes and no random paid rewards.

Payments are handled entirely by Google on Android and entirely by Apple on iPhone and iPad. We never see or store your name, payment card or billing details. The app receives only a confirmation from the store that a product was purchased (and, for the Admiral Pass, until when it is active), which is stored on your device so the item can be delivered. Unity IAP is a software library inside the app that talks to the store on your device; we run no server that receives your purchase data. Google's handling of your data is described in Google's Privacy Policy, and Apple's in Apple's Privacy Policy.

You can restore the Starter pack, Remove ads and an active Admiral Pass with Restore purchases in the Store tab of the Hangar, using the same Google account or Apple Account you bought with. You manage and cancel the Admiral Pass in your Google Play or App Store account settings, not in the game (see our Terms of Use).

7. Device permissions

Lodestar does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or files, and the current version does not send notifications. On iPhone and iPad, the only permission prompt is Apple's tracking prompt (section 5). On Android, the app uses standard permissions for internet access, in-app billing and the advertising ID, which do not show a prompt.

8. When you email us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message (for example your device model, Android or iOS version and app version). We use this only to answer you and to fix problems, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support. We do not use it for marketing and do not share it, except with our email provider, which stores the messages.

9. Children

Lodestar is made for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the corresponding minimum age in your country). The game contains science-fiction combat: space battles with ships and explosions, and first-person corridor shooting of alien creatures, with no blood beyond sci-fi creature effects and no real-money gambling. The app itself collects no personal information and has no account, chat or sharing with other players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information (for example by email), please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection apply, the processing described here relies on:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) – for personalised advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising purposes, collected through the consent message and, on iPhone and iPad, the ATT prompt. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) – for providing the game and for delivering, renewing and restoring what you purchase.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) – for showing non-personalised ads that fund a free game, for fraud prevention by the ad provider, and for answering support emails.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) – where we must keep records required by law.

16. Retention

  • Game data on your device: kept until you uninstall the app.
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed to resolve your request, and generally deleted within 24 months, unless the law requires longer retention.
  • Advertising data processed by Google: kept according to Google's own retention policies.
  • Purchase and subscription records held by Google or Apple: kept according to their own policies.

17. International transfers

We are based in Vietnam. Google may process ad-related data in the United States and other countries. For transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google relies on recognised safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and/or Standard Contractual Clauses. Support emails are stored by our email provider, which may also process data in other countries.

18. Security

Because we run no servers and collect no account data, the main safeguard is that your game data never leaves your device through us. We keep the app and its third-party SDKs up to date, request only the permissions we need (on iPhone and iPad, the tracking prompt is the only one), and protect the email account we use for support with strong authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the small amount of data involved.

19. Deleting your data

  • Game data: uninstall Lodestar from your device. Because the data exists only on your device, this deletes all progress, settings and local purchase records. The Starter pack, Remove ads and an active Admiral Pass can be restored later with Restore purchases; progress and Lumen Cores cannot be recovered.
  • Subscription: uninstalling the app does not cancel the Admiral Pass. Cancel it in your Google Play or App Store account settings.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device). On iPhone and iPad, turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or deny tracking for Lodestar, in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Ad choices: change them with the Ad privacy button in the Store tab of the Hangar (where a consent choice applies).
  • Data processed by Google: handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, where you can also exercise your rights with Google.
  • Emails: ask us to delete your correspondence at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.
