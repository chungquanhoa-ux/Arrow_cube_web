Lodestar: Galactic Fleet War

Humanity's last fleet is running out of stars. You are Captain Noor Calder, commander of the mothership Lodestar – and the Ilume carriers are coming.

Command two ways, switch any time

You need both: the Wick can't destroy a carrier alone, and the Lodestar can't clear a swarm without its fighters.

Pilot the Lodestar

A heavy flagship with turrets, a fighter swarm and the Lance main cannon. Break enemy shields, wreck subsystems, then fire the Lance into the core.

Launch the Wick

A fast, agile fighter with multi-target missile locks. Hunt enemy fighters and strike weak points the mothership can't reach.

14 missions across real stars

From Mars to Proxima Centauri, Tau Ceti, Gliese 876, Vega and the Kuiper Gate, all the way to Ashfold, the Ilume home world.

Every mission has its own rules

EMP storms, lightning, ice rings, twin carriers, locked cores – with cinematic briefings and video calls with your crew.

Fight on board

When enemies breach Deck 3, take a rifle and lead the defense yourself in first-person corridor battles, or send the marines and stay on the bridge.

Build your fleet

Earn Salvage, upgrade turrets, point defense, laser cooling and missiles, and unlock six ships, each with its own strengths.

Choices that matter

Story choices lead to different endings.

Kind to your device

Built for phones and tablets, with adaptive quality that keeps your device cool.

Fair to play

  • No energy timers, no loot boxes. Play as long as you like. Nothing you can buy gives a random reward.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. Watch one only when you want a reward or a second chance.
  • Never in the middle of a battle. Interstitial ads may appear now and then between missions, never during a fight. The Remove ads pack and the Admiral Pass switch them off.
  • Optional in-app purchases. Lumen Cores packs, a Starter pack, Remove ads and the monthly Admiral Pass subscription, through Google Play or the App Store.
  • No account, saved on your device. There is no sign-in and no cloud save. Where a consent choice applies, you can change how ads use your data at any time with the in-game Ad privacy button.
