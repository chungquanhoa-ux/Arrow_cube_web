Support & FAQ

Need a hand on the bridge, Captain? Find quick answers below, or write to us – we read every message.

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Playing the game

How is my progress saved?

The game saves your progress automatically on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so nothing is stored on our servers (we have none). If you uninstall the app, your progress is deleted too. Depending on your device settings, your device's own backup (on Android by Google, on iPhone and iPad for example iCloud) may include app data and can bring it back when you restore a device from that backup.

Do I need an internet connection?

The game has no servers of its own and saves everything on your device. Ads, purchases, the Admiral Pass and Restore purchases need an internet connection.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings on the title screen or from the pause menu, then pick one of the 10 languages: English, Tiếng Việt, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Deutsch, Français, Русский or Português (Brasil). The game starts in English until you choose another language.

What are Salvage and Lumen Cores?

Salvage is earned by playing missions and is used for upgrades. Lumen Cores are earned in play and can also be bought in packs. Neither has any cash value.

Is the game suitable for younger players?

Lodestar is made for players aged 13 and over. It contains science-fiction combat – space battles with ships and explosions, and first-person corridor shooting of alien creatures – with no blood beyond sci-fi creature effects, and no real-money gambling.

Where can I see the credits and licences?

Open the Hangar and choose the Credits tab. It lists the third-party 3D models and music (CC BY 4.0) and sound effects (CC0) used in the game.

Purchases and the Admiral Pass

What can I buy?

Everything is optional: Lumen Cores packs (consumable), the Starter pack and Remove ads (one-time, non-consumable), and the Admiral Pass, a monthly auto-renewing subscription. There are no loot boxes and no random paid rewards. See the Terms of Use for details.

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure you are online and signed in with the same Google account (Android) or Apple Account (iPhone and iPad) you used to buy. Then tap Restore purchases in the Store tab of the Hangar. The Starter pack, Remove ads and an active Admiral Pass are restored. Lumen Cores are consumable and cannot be restored.

I bought something but it did not appear.

Check your connection and tap Restore purchases. If a payment is still pending, or (on iPhone and iPad) waiting for a parent's approval through Ask to Buy, the item is delivered once it is completed. Still missing? Email us with the date of purchase, the item name and the order number from your store receipt – never send your card details.

How do I cancel the Admiral Pass?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not in the game. On Android: open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions (see Google Play Help). On iPhone and iPad: open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions (see Apple Support). Uninstalling the game does not cancel the subscription. After you cancel, the benefits last until the end of the period you have paid for.

How do I get a refund?

On Android, purchases are processed by Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google according to Google Play's refund policy.

On iPhone and iPad, purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple according to its policies. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads, privacy and data

Do I have to watch ads?

Rewarded ads are always optional: an ad plays only when you tap a watch button, and the reward is shown first. Interstitial ads may appear now and then between missions, after the results screen, but never during a battle. There are no banner ads.

How do I remove ads?

Buy the Remove ads pack or subscribe to the Admiral Pass in the Store tab of the Hangar. Either one switches off interstitial ads and gives you the rewards of rewarded ads without watching an ad.

The watch-ad button says no ad is available.

Ads need an internet connection, there is a daily limit, and sometimes no ad is available in your region at that moment. Please try again later.

How do I change my ad consent, tracking choice or advertising ID?

If a consent choice applies in your region (for example in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), tap the Ad privacy button in the Store tab of the Hangar to review or change it; this works the same on Android, iPhone and iPad. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device). On iPhone and iPad, tracking is controlled by Apple: go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Without consent (or, on iPhone and iPad, tracking permission), ads are non-personalised. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Lodestar has no account and keeps everything on your device, so uninstalling the app deletes all progress, settings and local data. Ad data is handled by Google: to reset it, reset or delete your advertising ID on Android, or use the tracking settings on iPhone and iPad. If you have emailed us and want your messages deleted, just ask.
