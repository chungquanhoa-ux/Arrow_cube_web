Support & FAQ

Need a hand on the island? Find quick answers below, or write to us – we read every message.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Playing the game

How is my progress saved?

The game saves your progress automatically on your device as you play. There is no account and no cloud save, so nothing is stored on our servers (we have none). If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too. On iPhone and iPad, your device's own backup (for example an iCloud backup) may include app data and can carry it to a new device when you restore from that backup. On Android, your device's backup may likewise include app data, under Google's terms.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole island, your home, fishing, villagers and your progress work without a connection. Only optional ads, purchases and Restore purchases need the internet.

How do I change the language?

Open the pause menu (or tap the gear button), choose Settings, then tap Language. Pick one of the 10 languages – English, Tiếng Việt, 简体中文, Français, Deutsch, 日本語, Português (Brasil), Español, Italiano or العربية – or choose Follow the device.

How do I earn gold?

Help villagers with their requests, take Maren's daily mail round, sell or cook the fish you catch, and collect your neighbours' gifts at home each day. You can also choose to watch an optional ad for a small “Gift of the wind”. Gold cannot be bought with real money.

Where can I see the credits and licences?

Open Settings › Credits & Licenses (also in the pause menu). It lists the third-party models, textures, music, sounds, fonts and icons used in the game and their licences (such as CC0 and CC BY).

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

On iPhone and iPad: make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, and that you are online. Then tap Restore purchases in the Stable or in the Home shop. All your purchases – horses, the herd bundle, Sunset furniture and outfits – are restored, whichever button you use.

On Android: your purchases are restored automatically when the game starts, as long as you use the same Google account you used to buy on Google Play. You can also tap Restore purchases in the same places.

I bought something but it did not appear.

Check your connection and tap Restore purchases. If a purchase was interrupted, the item is also delivered the next time you open the game online. On iPhone and iPad, if In-App Purchases are blocked in Screen Time, purchases cannot be completed. Still missing? Email us with the date of purchase and the item name – never send your card details.

How do I get a refund?

On iPhone and iPad, purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple according to its policies. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

On Android, purchases are processed by Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google according to Google Play's refund policy.

Ads, privacy and data

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Saltwind Isle uses only optional rewarded ads: an ad plays only when you tap a watch button, and the reward is shown first. There are no banners and no ads that start by themselves.

The watch-ad button says no ad is available.

Ads need an internet connection, there is a daily limit, and sometimes no ad is available in your region at that moment. Please try again later. Your game is never blocked by this.

How do I change my ad consent, tracking choice or advertising ID?

If a consent choice applies in your region (for example in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), open Settings › Privacy options in the game to review or change it; this works the same on iPhone, iPad and Android. On iPhone and iPad, tracking is controlled by Apple: go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android there is no tracking prompt; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most devices). Without consent (or, on iPhone and iPad, tracking permission), ads are non-personalised. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Return reminders are currently available on iPhone and iPad only; the Android version does not send reminders. On iPhone and iPad, use Return reminders in the game's Settings, where you can also set quiet hours, or go to iOS Settings › Notifications › Saltwind Isle. Reminders are created on your device, at most one a day, and stop after a few if you do not return.

Why does the game ask for access to Photos? Where are images saved on Android?

On iPhone and iPad: only when you tap Save to Photos for a postcard, photo or clip. The game asks for add-only access: it can save images to your library but cannot see or read your existing photos. If you declined, you can allow it later in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

On Android: tapping Save adds the image to your device gallery (Pictures/Saltwind Isle) on Android 10 and later, without asking for any storage permission. On Android 8 and 9, saving is not available. Sharing uses the Android share sheet. Screen-clip recording is available on iPhone and iPad only.

How do I delete my data?

Saltwind Isle has no account and keeps everything on your device. Deleting the app removes all progress, settings and local data. To reset ad tracking, use your device's tracking settings on iPhone and iPad, or reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings on Android. If you have emailed us and want your messages deleted, just ask.
