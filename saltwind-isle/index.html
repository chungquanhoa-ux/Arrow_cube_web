Saltwind Isle: Cozy Life Sim

Step off the ferry onto a small island where the wind is different every day. Wander the meadows, ride along the shore, fish off the pier, help your neighbours and make a little home your own – at your own pace.

Island life, one breeze at a time

No timers to beat and nothing lost while you are away – just the island, the wind and the people who live there.

Roam an open island

Walk or run across meadows, pinewoods, beaches and cliffs, ride your horse, or take a bicycle, a motorbike or a car.

A home of your own

Grow a timber cabin into a two-storey villa in a Nordic, European, Japanese or Vietnamese style, then furnish and decorate it.

Fishing & fish book

Twelve species to catch. What bites depends on the time of day, the season, the wind and where you stand.

Villages & villagers

A fishing village at the ferry landing, a Vietnamese-style village and a Japanese-style village – with neighbours who ask for a hand and a daily mail round.

Wardrobe

Choose your islander, try on outfits, hats and capes, and change your look whenever you like.

Photo mode & postcards

Pose, pick a filter and a frame, and turn your favourite views into postcards for your album.

Day, night & weather

Sunrise to starlight, rain and changing winds that move the grass, the windmills and the sea.

Raise a flag

Fly the island's own flag from the flagpole, or unlock flags from around the world.

Fair and friendly

  • Plays offline. No account and no login – your progress is saved on your device.
  • Ads only when you choose. Ads are optional rewarded videos that you start yourself for a reward that is shown before you watch.
  • Optional one-time purchases. Some horses, furniture and outfits can be bought through the App Store or Google Play; they are yours to keep and can be restored. No subscriptions and no random rewards.
  • Your privacy choices are respected. Where required, you can choose how ads use your data, and change your mind later in Settings.
