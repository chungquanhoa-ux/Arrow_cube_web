Privacy Policy

Saltwind Isle: Cozy Life Sim · App ID com.chung.saltwindisle
Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short

  • Saltwind Isle plays offline. There is no account, no login and no chat, and we run no servers that receive your data.
  • Your progress, settings and purchased items are saved only on your device.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location, and we have added no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • Optional rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may process some device and usage data to show and measure ads, according to the choices you make in the consent message and, on iPhone and iPad, in Apple's tracking prompt.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who is responsible

Saltwind Isle: Cozy Life Sim (“Saltwind Isle”, “the app”) is developed and published by DOLOTECH TECHNOLOGY CO., based in Vietnam (“we”, “us”). We are the controller for the limited processing described in this policy that is under our control. You can reach us at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Saltwind Isle app for iPhone and iPad (distributed through the App Store), to the Saltwind Isle app for Android devices running Android 8.0 or later (distributed by us through Google Play, package com.chung.saltwindisle) and to this website. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts.

2. Overview and what we do not collect

Saltwind Isle is designed to work without us knowing who you are. We do not ask for, collect or store:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there is no account or login);
  • your contacts, camera, microphone or files, and we never read your photo library or gallery;
  • your precise location;
  • chat messages or user-generated content shared with other players (the game has no chat and no online multiplayer);
  • analytics, usage statistics or crash reports – the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following locally on your device only: your progress (gold, home and furniture, horses, vehicles, outfits, fish book, villager friendships and requests, flags), your postcard album, your settings (such as language, sound, music, graphics, controls, reminders and quiet hours), a local record of items you have purchased, and the times of day you usually open the game (used only on the device to time reminders). This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app. On iPhone and iPad, depending on your device settings, your device's own backup (for example an iCloud backup) may include app data; such backups are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy, not by us. On Android, your device's backup may likewise include app data; such backups are governed by Google's terms and privacy policy, not by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Saltwind Isle is free to play and is supported by optional ads delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited (“Google”).

How ads appear

  • The app uses rewarded ads only. An ad plays only when you tap a button to watch one, and the reward is shown before you choose – for example a little extra gold (“Gift of the wind”), unlocking a Wind-blossom furniture edition, or unlocking a country flag for the flagpole.
  • There are no banner ads, no interstitial ads and no ads that start by themselves. You can enjoy the whole game without watching any ad.
  • Ad views are limited per day. Ads load only when your device is online; offline, the game continues to work normally.

What Google may process

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers – on iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, otherwise the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and other non-tracking identifiers permitted by Apple; on Android, the Android Advertising ID (AAID), which you can reset or delete in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most devices);
  • your IP address and an approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data, such as ads shown, taps and video completions;
  • device and app information and diagnostics, such as device model, operating-system version, language, app version, and ad performance and crash data of the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, to personalise ads where you have consented, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google acts under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Consent, tracking and privacy options

  • Consent message (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): Before ads are requested, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You can accept, reject or choose individual purposes and vendors. Where a US state privacy message applies, the same platform lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Apple tracking prompt (ATT, iPhone and iPad): The app asks for permission to track only through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The IDFA is available only if you choose “Allow”. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: There is no ATT prompt on Android. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may use the Android Advertising ID (AAID). You can reset or delete it at any time in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most devices).
  • Non-personalised ads: If you do not give consent, or (on iPhone and iPad) you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalised based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which rely on context such as the app and your approximate location.
  • Change your mind: Where a consent choice applies to you, a Privacy options button appears in the game's Settings so you can review or change your choices at any time. It works the same way on iPhone, iPad and Android.

6. In-app purchases

Saltwind Isle offers optional, non-consumable purchases through the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and through Google Play Billing (Android): horses (Mist, Coal, Honey and White Wind), a bundle of the whole herd, Sunset-edition furniture pieces and the complete Sunset set, and special outfits. There are no subscriptions, no consumable purchases and no loot boxes. The in-game currency, gold, cannot be bought with real money.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple on iPhone and iPad, and entirely by Google on Android. We never see or store your name, payment card or billing details. The app receives only a confirmation from Apple or Google that a product was purchased, which is stored on your device so the item can be unlocked. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy, and Google's in Google's Privacy Policy. You can restore your purchases with Restore purchases in the Stable or in the Home shop. On Android, purchases are also restored automatically when the game starts, as long as you use the same Google account.

7. Local notifications

Return reminders are currently available on iPhone and iPad only; the Android version does not send reminders.

On iPhone and iPad, if you allow notifications, Saltwind Isle can show optional, gentle reminders after you have been away for a while (about 1, 3, 7 and 14 days – at most one per day, and then they stop until you play again). Reminders never arrive during your quiet hours (22:00–08:00 by default, adjustable in Settings). They are scheduled on your device by the app itself – they are not sent from a server, no notification token is shared with us, and no additional data is collected. You can switch them off at any time with Return reminders in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings › Notifications.

8. Photos, postcards and screen clips

  • On iPhone and iPad, when you tap Save to Photos for a postcard or photo, the app asks for Apple's add-only photo permission. It can add images to your library but cannot see, read or upload your existing photos. Permission is requested only when you choose to save.
  • On Android 10 and later, tapping Save adds the image to your device gallery (Pictures/Saltwind Isle) without asking for any storage permission. On Android 8 and 9, saving is not available.
  • If you share a postcard, the iOS share sheet (on Android, the Android share sheet) is used and you decide where it goes. Nothing is uploaded by us.
  • On iPhone and iPad, if you record a short gameplay clip, it is recorded on your device with Apple's ReplayKit with the microphone switched off, and saved to your Photos only when you choose. Screen-clip recording is not available on Android.

9. When you email us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message (for example your device model, iOS or Android version and app version). We use this only to answer you and to fix problems, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support. We do not use it for marketing and do not share it, except with our email provider, which stores the messages.

10. Children

On the App Store, Saltwind Isle has an age rating of 4+. On Google Play, its content rating is assigned through the IARC rating questionnaire (the game contains no violence and includes digital purchases), and the app is aimed at a general audience aged 13 and over. Its content is suitable for players of all ages. It is made for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13 (or the corresponding minimum age in your country). The app itself collects no personal information and has no account, chat or sharing with other players. Ad requests are configured for a general audience, and when consent (or, on iPhone and iPad, tracking permission) is not given, ads are non-personalised. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information (for example by email), please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection apply, the processing described here relies on:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) – for personalised advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising purposes, collected through the consent message and, on iPhone and iPad, the ATT prompt; and, on iPhone and iPad, for notifications and adding images to your photo library. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) – for providing the game and delivering and restoring the items you purchase.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) – for showing non-personalised ads that fund a free game, for fraud prevention by the ad provider, and for answering support emails.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) – where we must keep records required by law.

  • Game data on your device: kept until you delete the app.
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed to resolve your request, and generally deleted within 24 months, unless the law requires longer retention.
  • Advertising data processed by Google: kept according to Google's own retention policies.

18. International transfers

We are based in Vietnam. Google may process ad-related data in the United States and other countries. For transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google relies on recognised safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and/or Standard Contractual Clauses. Support emails are stored by our email provider, which may also process data in other countries.

19. Security

Because we run no servers and collect no account data, the main safeguard is that your game data never leaves your device through us. We keep the app and its third-party SDKs up to date, request only the permissions we need and only when you use the related feature (on iPhone and iPad: notifications, adding photos, tracking; on Android, saving images to the gallery needs no storage permission), and protect the email account we use for support with strong authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the small amount of data involved.

20. Deleting your data

  • Game data: delete Saltwind Isle from your device. All progress, settings and local purchase records are removed. Purchased items can be restored later with Restore purchases (on Android also automatically when the game starts); progress cannot be recovered.
  • Advertising ID: on iPhone and iPad, turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or deny tracking for Saltwind Isle, in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most devices).
  • Ad choices: change them with Privacy options in the game's Settings (where a consent choice applies).
  • Emails: ask us to delete your correspondence at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.
