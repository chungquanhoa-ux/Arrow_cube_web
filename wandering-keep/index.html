Wandering Keep: Tower Defense

A gentle giant carries your little village on its back. Build towers around its deck and keep every house safe on a long journey across six wild lands.

Drop towers onto the eight posts around your creature’s deck, upgrade them by dropping the same tower of the same level on top, wake synergies between neighbouring posts, pick relics along the road and choose your pace: a calm stroll or a brave march. Every journey plays out a little differently.

What awaits on the road

  • A village on a giantFour little houses and your banner ride on a round wooden deck. Foes climb over the railing to capture them, so keep every house safe.
  • Eight postsEach post around the railing holds one tower. Drop the same tower of the same level on top to upgrade it up to level 3, or move, swap and sell your towers.
  • SynergiesPut different towers on neighbouring posts to wake glowing synergies. Each one you find is written in your Synergy Book.
  • RelicsChoose relics such as the Crystal Lens, the Thunder Bell or a Rain Cloud in a Jar to shape each run.
  • Six landsThe Amazon Rainforest, the Alps, the Western Canyons, the Nordic Fjords, the Mediterranean Ruins and Ha Long Bay, each with its own guardian boss.
  • Six creaturesMossback Tortoise, Woolly Mammoth, Elder Stag, Lantern Snail, Sky Whale and Great Sea Turtle, which carries your village across the sea of Ha Long Bay. Each moves in its own way and has its own special skill.
  • 36 stages and moreA new Daily Journey every day, on the same route for every traveller, plus a Weekly Boss to defeat for a big gem reward.
  • Your paceStroll to rest, heal the village and get a free tower, or march to face more enemies for more gold. Towers fire on their own; you plan the build.
  • Fair and cosyNo account needed and your progress stays on your device. Purchases are optional, and every creature and upgrade can be earned by playing.
