Support and FAQ

Need a hand on the road? Most answers are below. If you are still stuck, write to us at dolotech.vn@gmail.com.

Tip: Settings opens from the gear button at the top of the main camp screen.

Playing the game

How do I play?
  1. Choose the creature that will carry your village. In Ha Long Bay, the Great Sea Turtle always carries it across the open sea.
  2. Drag a tower from the tray onto a glowing empty post around the deck railing. Each of the 8 posts holds one tower. The reroll button in the tray gives you new offers for gold.
  3. Drop a tower onto the same tower of the same level to upgrade it (up to level 3). Put different towers on neighbouring posts to wake synergies (glowing links). Touch a built tower and drag it to move it, swap it with another tower or sell it.
  4. Foes climb over the railing and attack the nearest house. Protect the 4 houses: if all of them are captured, the journey ends (you can revive once by watching an optional video). Towers fire on their own; tap the round skill button when things get tough.
  5. Between waves, choose your pace: Stroll (costs food, gives a free tower and heals the village) or March (more enemies, more gold), and pick relics when they are offered.
  6. Defeat the guardian boss of a land to open the next one. There are 36 stages across six lands.
What are amber and gems?

They are in-game currencies used for upgrades and unlocks. You earn them by playing: journeys, the Daily Reward, the Free Wheel, the Travel Chest, the Daily Journey and the Weekly Boss. Gems can also be bought in the Shop, but purchases are always optional.

What are the Daily Journey and the Weekly Boss?

The Daily Journey is a new route each day, the same for every traveller; try to beat your best score, and the first run each day gives a gem bonus. The Weekly Boss is a mighty guardian that blocks the road all week; defeat it once for a big gem reward.

Can I play offline?

Yes, you can play without an internet connection. Rewarded videos, purchases and restoring purchases need a connection.

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings → Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchases in the Shop. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy, and that you are online.

This restores permanent items: Remove Ads and the Sky Whale. Gems and amber from gem packs and the Starter Pack are added to your progress when you buy them and cannot be restored after uninstalling.

I bought something but did not receive it.

Some payments take a moment to be confirmed by the store. Keep the game open for a minute, then restart it; the item is delivered once the store confirms. If it still does not arrive, email us with your order number (from your Apple receipt email or your Google Play order history).

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by the store, not by us. Request a refund through Apple or Google Play.

Ads, privacy and reminders

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change your consent at any time. The button appears where privacy rules require it, such as in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states.

On iPhone and iPad you can also allow or stop tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Google or privacy settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

When do ads appear?

Rewarded videos play only when you tap to watch one for a reward. An ad may appear occasionally after a journey ends, never in the middle of a battle. The Remove Ads purchase turns those off; rewarded videos stay available if you want them.

How do I turn off reminders?

Switch off Settings → Reminders in the game. You can also turn notifications off in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Notifications → Wandering Keep; Android: Settings → Apps → Wandering Keep → Notifications). Reminders are created on your device; there is no push server.

Language and saved progress

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Language and pick one of the 10 languages: English, Português (Brasil), Tiếng Việt, 简体中文, Français, Deutsch, 日本語, Español, Italiano or العربية. Choose System language to follow your device.

Is my progress saved online? Can I move it to a new phone?

No. There is no account and no cloud save: your progress is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress permanently, and we cannot recover it or move it between devices. Permanent purchases can be restored on a new device with Restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game; this deletes all game data from your device. For ad data, use Settings → Ad privacy choices and Google’s controls described in the Privacy Policy. To delete emails you sent us, just ask.

Contact us

Email dolotech.vn@gmail.com. To help us help you quickly, please include:

  • device model (for example iPhone 13 or Samsung Galaxy A54);
  • operating system version (for example iOS 18.1 or Android 15);
  • game version, shown at the bottom of the Settings screen (for example v1.0.0);
  • what happened and the steps to repeat it, with a screenshot or short video if you can;
  • for purchase problems, the order number or transaction ID.

Please never send passwords or payment card details. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.
