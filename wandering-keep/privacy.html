Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wandering Keep: Tower Defense (the “Game”, app ID com.chung.wanderingkeep) for iOS and Android handles information. The Game is developed and published by DOLOTECH, the brand of an independent developer based in Vietnam (“we”, “us”, “our”).

The short version

  • There is no account and no login. We do not collect your name, email address or other personal data in the Game.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.
  • We do not use any analytics SDK of our own and we run no game servers.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, your IP address, approximate location derived from it and ad interaction data. You decide about personalised ads.
  • Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are optional.

1. Who is responsible

DOLOTECH is the controller for the limited processing described here that we decide on. Contact: dolotech.vn@gmail.com. Google, Apple and Google Play act as independent controllers for the data they process under their own privacy policies, which we link to below.

2. What we do not collect

  • No account, login, profile, name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own, and no servers of ours that receive your gameplay.
  • No payment card or bank details.

3. Data stored on your device

The Game saves, in its private storage on your device: your progress (stages, creatures, upgrades, amber, gems, Synergy Book), your settings (music, sound, vibration, reminders, language), permanent purchases you own (such as Remove Ads) and the status of your ad consent. This data is not sent to us and we cannot access it. It stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google:

  • Rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch one, for example to revive, double a reward, open a chest early or get an extra spin of the Free Wheel.
  • Interstitial ads appear occasionally between journeys, never in the middle of a battle. The optional Remove Ads purchase turns them off. Rewarded videos stay available if you want them.

To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect or receive:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the Android advertising ID, subject to your consent choices and device settings);
  • your IP address, and an approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data, such as ads shown, views, taps and whether a video was completed;
  • general device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version.

Google uses this data to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, only where you have agreed, to personalise ads. Ad content in the Game is limited to a family-friendly (PG) maturity rating. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Consent and ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a consent message based on the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). You can accept, refuse or choose purposes and partners. If you refuse personalisation, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data (such as IP address for coarse location, frequency capping and fraud prevention).
  • United States: where US state privacy laws apply, a message lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information and of targeted advertising.
  • Change your mind any time: open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the Game. The button appears when these rules apply in your region.
  • iOS: Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the Game may track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device’s Google or privacy settings (the menu name varies by device, often Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can also review your Google ad settings at My Ad Center.

Whatever you choose, the Game stays free and plays the same.

6. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (gem packs, the Starter Pack, the Sky Whale creature and Remove Ads) are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your card details, billing address or store account name. The store gives the Game a receipt and transaction ID, which is checked on your device to deliver the item. We do not run a purchase server. “Restore purchases” asks the store for the permanent items linked to your store account.

7. Local notifications

If you allow them, the Game schedules gentle reminders on your device, for example when your travel chest is full or a new Daily Journey opens. They are created locally. There is no push server, and no notification token is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders in the Game, or in your device’s notification settings.

8. When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include (for example your device model, operating system version, game version or a screenshot). We use it only to answer you and fix problems. We do not use it for marketing and we do not share it, except where the law requires.

9. Legal bases

Where the GDPR, the UK GDPR or similar laws apply, the legal bases are:

  • Consent: personalised ads and access to device identifiers for advertising (including App Tracking Transparency on iOS). You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalised ads that keep the Game free, ad measurement and frequency capping, fraud and abuse prevention, and answering your support emails.
  • Contract: delivering and restoring the items you buy.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information by law.

10. Retention

  • On-device data is kept until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 24 months after our last exchange, unless the law requires longer.
  • Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policy, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

11. International transfers

We are based in Vietnam. Google processes advertising data on servers around the world, including in the United States. Google states that it relies on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK addendum and its certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. If you email us, your message is handled by our email provider and may be stored outside your country.

12. Security

Because there are no accounts and no servers of ours holding player data, there is very little data that could be exposed. Game data stays in the app’s private storage, protected by your operating system. Ads and store communications use encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to keep the Game safe.

13. Children

The Game is rated 9+ on the App Store and Everyone 10+ on Google Play. It is not directed to children and is not designed for children under 13. We do not target ads at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it. Parents can also limit ad tracking with the device settings above or buy Remove Ads.

14. Deleting your data

  • Game data: uninstall the Game, or clear its app data. This permanently deletes your progress and settings from the device. We cannot recover them.
  • Ad data: use Settings → Ad privacy choices, your device’s ad settings and Google’s controls (My Ad Center, Google data and privacy).
  • Emails: ask us at dolotech.vn@gmail.com and we will delete our correspondence with you.

15. Rights in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, you have the right to access your personal data, to rectification, to erasure, to restriction of processing, to object (including to processing based on legitimate interests), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office; in Switzerland, the FDPIC).

Because we hold no personal data about you except emails you send, most requests about ad data are best handled through Google’s tools above. We will help you with any request; write to us and we will reply within one month.

16. Rights in the United States

California (CCPA/CPRA)

In the last 12 months, the following categories of personal information may have been collected through the Game, from your device, by Google for advertising: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (ad interactions) and approximate geolocation derived from IP address. We do not collect sensitive personal information.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, letting Google use identifiers for personalised (cross-context behavioural) advertising may count as “selling” or “sharing” under California law. You can opt out at any time with Settings → Ad privacy choices in the Game, and on iOS by not allowing tracking. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

You have the right to know and access, to delete, to correct, to opt out of sale or sharing, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information (which we do not use). We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights: the Game, its prices and its features stay the same. You may use an authorised agent. We will respond within 45 days; because we hold no data linked to you, we may not be able to match a request to any data, and we will explain this if so.

Other US states

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have similar rights: to access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of their data, and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and profiling. Use Settings → Ad privacy choices or email us. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with “Appeal” in the subject line; if you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact your state Attorney General.

17. Rights in Brazil (LGPD)

For users in Brazil, the controller is DOLOTECH, and our communication channel for data subjects is dolotech.vn@gmail.com. The legal bases (art. 7 of Law 13,709/2018) are consent for personalised ads, legitimate interest for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention, and performance of a contract for purchases. Under art. 18 you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, portability, deletion of data processed with consent, information about entities we share data with, information about the possibility of refusing consent and its consequences, and withdrawal of consent. You may also file a complaint with the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD). See the Portuguese version for full details.

18. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. For significant changes we will also give notice in the Game or on this page, and ask for your consent again where the law requires.
